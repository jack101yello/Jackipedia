# The AdS/CFT Correspondence

The AdS/CFT Correspondence is a connection between theories of quantum gravity on asymptotically anti-de Sitter spacetimes and conformal field theories on their boundaries. The most famous and original manifestation of this is the correspondence between Type IIB superstring theory on $\text{AdS}_5 \times \text{S}^5$ and 4d $\mathcal{N} = 4$ super-Yang-Mills theory.

A weaker form of the correspondence related the semiclassical limit of the gravitational theory to the large $N$ limit of the conformal field theory.
