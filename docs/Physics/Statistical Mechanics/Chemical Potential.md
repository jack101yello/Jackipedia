
# Chemical Potential

For a statistical system, the chemical potential is the change in internal energy per change in number of particles for constant entropy and volume. That is to say, the internal energy of a statistical system is given by:

$$
dU = T dS - P dV + \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mu_i\: dN_i
$$

for $n$ species with chemical potentials $\mu_i$, respectively.

## Fugacity

For an ideal gas, the change in chemical potential is

$$
d\mu = RT\: d\log P
$$

For a real gas, this no longer holds. However, for a real gas with [[Fugacity|fugacity]] $f$, it holds that

$$
d\mu = RT\: d\log f
$$

Hence, the fugacity serves as an effective pressure with respect to the chemical potential.
