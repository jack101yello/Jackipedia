# Sierpinski Space

The Sierpinski space $S$ is a [[Topological Space|topological space]] consisting of two points, $0$, and $1$, with [[Topology|topology]] $\mathcal{T} = \{\emptyset, 1, S\}$.

## Category Theory

Both the [[Functor|functor]] which sends a topological space to its set of [[Open Set|open sets]] and that the functor which sends a topological space to its set of [[Closed Set|closed sets]] are [[Representable Functor|representable functors]], and are in fact both represented by the Sierpinski space.
