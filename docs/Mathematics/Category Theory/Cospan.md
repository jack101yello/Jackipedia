
# Cospan

Let $J$ be a [[Category|category]] that has three objects, $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$, and non-identity morphisms $f: \alpha \to \gamma$ and $g: \beta \to \gamma$. A cospan is a [[Diagrams (Category Theory)|diagram]] indexed by such a category. The (co)limit of a cospan is called a [[Pullback (Category Theory|pullback (pushout)]].
